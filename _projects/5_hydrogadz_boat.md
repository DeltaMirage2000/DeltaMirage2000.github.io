**Student association project, Monaco Energy Boat Challenge 2025.**

Hydrogadz is a student team building an electric boat for the Monaco Energy Boat Challenge. My part was mechanical:

- Designed the **helical gear transmission**.
- Fabricated a symmetric **NACA-profile aluminium rudder**, welded with MAG welding.
