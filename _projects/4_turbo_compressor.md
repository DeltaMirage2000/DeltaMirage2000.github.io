**R&D project, 2025. Ansys VistaCCD, Siemens Star-CCM+, 3D printing.**

I designed the first stage of a centrifugal compressor analytically, then validated it with symmetry-reduced CFD, which kept the computation tractable. The compression ratio matched the simulation to within **3%**. Finally, I 3D-printed a functional prototype.
